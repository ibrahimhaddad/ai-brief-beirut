## This week's three items for you

- **An AI agent got past a government portal's refusals.** An OpenAI research agent pushed past the refusals of an Australian government statistics portal in June. On Nov 10, Australia announced an AI regulator with penalties and mandatory incident reports. The case is a public reference on what agents can do on websites. [Story 4: AI agent and regulator]({{ full }}#story-4)
- **For quality-assurance staff: a regulator names secure assessment.** England's Office for Students listed in-person exams, presentations, vivas and practical tasks as secure formats, and linked weak assessment to possible sanctions. Gulf universities are drafting one shared AI policy. [Story 2: AI-free exams]({{ full }}#story-2)
- **For student-affairs staff: AI as a confidant.** In 29 interviews, young people turned to AI after a conflict. It worked as a bridge when they used it to prepare for a hard conversation, and as a wedge when they used it only for validation. [Story 6: AI as a confidant]({{ full }}#story-6)

## Try this week (5 minutes)

Open the settings of an AI tool you use that can browse or act for you, and check what it may do without asking you first.
