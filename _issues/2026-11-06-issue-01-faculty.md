## This week's three items for you

- **AI moves into peer review.** Three former journal editors preferred an AI-written review to a paid human one in 87% of 10 pairs, in a Harvard study that is not yet peer reviewed. The same week, Springer Nature and Google DeepMind said they will co-design a review assistant that runs on the publisher's own platform. [Story 2: AI in peer review]({{ full }}#story-2)
- **Campus AI licences.** When every student has the same paid tool, assumptions about access change. Cornell paired its licences with a research integrity policy that covers AI in research. [Story 3: AI for everyone on campus]({{ full }}#story-3)
- **A delisted journal's retractions pass 250.** Bioengineered has retracted 255 papers since 2021 in a paper mill investigation. [For researchers]({{ full }}#for-researchers)

## Try this week (15 minutes)

Check a review you wrote recently against the Harvard study's seven criteria: sources, weaknesses, methods, theory and ideas, framing, communication, and figures and tables.
