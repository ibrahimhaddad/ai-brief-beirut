## This week's three items for you

- **AI agents that act on their own are arriving.** They stay connected to email, calendars and files and keep working between requests, so the first question is what an agent can touch and who signs off. In regulated sectors, where customer data goes may decide whether one can be used at all. [Story 4]({{ full }}#story-4)
- **Small businesses pick AI tools that feel safe and simple.** Gulf merchants in one study used general AI tools mostly for marketing copy, and none had tried them for sales analytics. A survey found 30% of UAE and Saudi shoppers fully trust AI recommendations. [Story 5]({{ full }}#story-5)
- **Critical PHP flaws were patched on 21 October, and attackers moved first.** Online shops, booking sites and older company websites are in scope. The fixed versions are 8.2.35, 8.3.36, 8.4.27 and 8.5.12 or later. [Story 1]({{ full }}#story-1)

Compute in the region: the UAE says its $1.4 trillion US technology pledge is ahead of schedule. It could mean more local compute over time; timing and pricing are not public. [Regional AI investment]({{ full }}#investment)

## Try this week (10 minutes)

List three tasks in your business that repeat every week and involve no customer data. They are the lower-risk place to test an AI tool first.
