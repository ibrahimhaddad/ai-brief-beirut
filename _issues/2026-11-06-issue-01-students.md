## This week's two items for you

- **AI for everyone on campus.** Princeton made ChatGPT Plus, normally $20 a month, free for its students, and Cornell will open several tools to undergraduates in early 2027. A university-licensed account and a free personal one carry different data terms, including whether your chats train the model. Before uploading coursework, check which kind you are using. [Story 3: AI for everyone on campus]({{ full }}#story-3)
- **An open challenge.** The Global Sustainability Challenge, led by Stanford's Doerr School of Sustainability, takes bachelor, master and PhD students worldwide in teams of 1 to 6, with cash prizes. Deadline Nov 22. [Opportunities and deadlines]({{ full }}#opportunities)

## Try this week (10 minutes)

Ask an AI tool to critique a paragraph you wrote, then check one claim it makes against a source you trust.
