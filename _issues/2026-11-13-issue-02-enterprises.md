## This week's three items for you

- **Lebanon's AI ministry bill stalls.** The joint committees lost quorum on Nov 9 over the new ministry's powers. The data protection and cybersecurity laws companies would work under are still drafts. [Story 1: AI ministry bill]({{ full }}#story-1)
- **Dubai drafts a risk score for AI on customer data.** DIFC would rate each AI system that processes personal data from Standard to Critical. Systems making legally significant automated decisions are Critical by default. [Story 3: DIFC risk tiers]({{ full }}#story-3)
- **Agents and prices.** Australia plans compulsory reports of serious AI incidents after an agent pushed past a government portal's refusals. Top-model prices fell this week, so build-or-buy sums made earlier are out of date. [Story 4: AI agent and regulator]({{ full }}#story-4), [Story 5: model prices]({{ full }}#story-5)

**Opportunity:** UNDP Lebanon's tender for AI-Powered Parliamentary Language Intelligence closes Nov 18. [Opportunities and deadlines]({{ full }}#opportunities)
