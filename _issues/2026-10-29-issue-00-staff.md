## This week's two items for you

- **If your unit runs a website, form or registration page, check it.** Two critical PHP flaws found with AI help were patched on 21 October, and attackers moved first. Ask IT or your vendor whether PHP is at 8.2.35, 8.3.36, 8.4.27, 8.5.12 or later. [Story 1]({{ full }}#story-1)
- **AI agents that act on their own are arriving.** Anthropic's Claude Duet is US only for now, so US-based colleagues may see it first. Ask your IT team before connecting any agent to a work account. [Story 4]({{ full }}#story-4)

For advising and career services: 48% of 2,500 US employers say AI makes degrees more important. Quote it with its US scope. [Story 2]({{ full }}#story-2)

## Try this week (10 minutes)

List every website, form and sign-up page your unit owns and who maintains each one. Send the list to IT with the PHP question.
