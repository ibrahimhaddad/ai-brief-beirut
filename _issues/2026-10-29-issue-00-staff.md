## This week's two items for you

- **Critical PHP flaws were patched on 21 October, and attackers moved first.** AI helped find them. University registration pages, forms and older sites are in scope. The fixed versions are 8.2.35, 8.3.36, 8.4.27 and 8.5.12 or later. [Story 1]({{ full }}#story-1)
- **AI agents that act on their own are arriving.** Anthropic's Claude Duet is US only for now, so US-based colleagues may see it first. An agent with standing access to a work account falls under an employer's rules on outside services. [Story 4]({{ full }}#story-4)

For advising and career services: 48% of 2,500 US employers say AI makes degrees more important. The figure is US only. [Story 2]({{ full }}#story-2)

## Try this week (10 minutes)

Read [CERT-EU's advisory on the PHP flaws](https://cert.europa.eu/publications/security-advisories/2026-018/). It sets out the risk and the fixed versions in plain terms.
