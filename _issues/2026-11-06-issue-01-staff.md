## This week's two items for you

- **Universities buy AI for everyone.** Cornell is giving faculty, staff and PhD students several AI tools from November under a two-year pilot, and Princeton is offering ChatGPT. Both say data will not train the models. Cornell also requires an orientation, plus a primer before agentic tools. The terms apply first to US-based readers, and they are a ready comparison for any AI contract. [Story 3: AI for everyone on campus]({{ full }}#story-3)
- **PHP2Shell is now confirmed in attacks.** For staff who run websites: CISA lists both flaws as exploited, and one campaign took over 25 firewalls by chaining PHP2Shell with a Linux flaw. The fixed versions are unchanged. [Story 6: PHP2Shell attacks]({{ full }}#story-6)

## Try this week (5 minutes)

Open the data controls in the AI tool you use most and see whether your chats are used for training.
