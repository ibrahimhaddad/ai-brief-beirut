## This week's two items for you
- **Writing for the detector.** A quarter of 5,034 US undergraduates said they changed how they write to avoid being falsely flagged by AI detectors. Keeping drafts and version history makes your process visible. [Story 4: Students and AI detectors]({{ full }}#story-4)
- **An AI course adviser in Qatar.** Qatar connected an AI academic adviser to its national student information system. Check any AI course-planning answer against your programme's published requirements. [Story 3: Qatar's AI adviser]({{ full }}#story-3)

**Opportunities:** MBZUAI takes master's and PhD applications until Dec 15, and the Create AI Agents Championship in Dubai, with a youth category, takes entries until January 2027. [Opportunities and deadlines]({{ full }}#opportunities)

## Try this week (5 minutes)

In Gemini or ChatGPT, check whether chat history and model training are switched on, and decide whether you want them. Gizmodo reports Gemini now keeps chats for about two months even with history off.
