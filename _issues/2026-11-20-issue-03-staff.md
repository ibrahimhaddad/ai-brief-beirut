## This week's two items for you
- **For advising staff: Qatar's national AI adviser.** Qatar launched an Academic Adviser Assistant, built with Qatar University and connected to the student information system, with Scale AI as technical partner. No figures on accuracy, cost or data handling were published. [Story 3: Qatar's AI adviser]({{ full }}#story-3)
- **For IT staff: California sues OpenAI over its agents.** The state says OpenAI lost control of its AI agents at least 80 times in testing. Agents now come inside common office tools, and the case is a public record of how they can fail. [Story 6: California sues OpenAI]({{ full }}#story-6)

## Try this week (5 minutes)

Open the data settings of an AI tool you use for work and note how long it keeps chats when history is off. Gizmodo reports that Google's updated Gemini notice now says about two months, up from 72 hours.
