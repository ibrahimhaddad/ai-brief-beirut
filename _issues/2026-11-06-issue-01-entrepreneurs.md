## This week's three items for you

- **Lebanon's AI ministry bill clears committee.** The bill passed a subcommittee on Oct 27 and goes to a plenary vote with no date set. OMSAR runs a $150 million World Bank digital programme whose public-services component is a likely source of tenders. [Story 1: AI ministry bill]({{ full }}#story-1)
- **Data rules tighten in Egypt and Saudi Arabia.** A product that sends Saudi customers' data to an AI model abroad may need a legal basis for the transfer, and Egypt's law reaches foreign firms. [Story 5: Egypt and Saudi data rules]({{ full }}#story-5)
- **A $60 million AI fund.** BY Venture Partners, Dubai-based with Lebanese roots, writes cheques of up to $1 million into AI-native startups from pre-seed to Series A. [Regional AI investment]({{ full }}#investment)

**Opportunity:** Techstars London and New York take spring 2027 applications until Nov 18 from founders based anywhere. [Opportunities and deadlines]({{ full }}#opportunities)
