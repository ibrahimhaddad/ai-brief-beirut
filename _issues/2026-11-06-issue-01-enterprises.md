## This week's three items for you

- **AI processed inside the UAE.** Abu Dhabi signed four OpenAI agreements with inference on UAE servers, and each check in its government programme traces back to a rule, with an official making the final call. [Story 4: UAE agents, data at home]({{ full }}#story-4)
- **Egypt and Saudi data rules.** Egypt's grace period ended around Nov 1, and Saudi draft rules would require personal data to be stored in the Kingdom. [Story 5: Egypt and Saudi data rules]({{ full }}#story-5)
- **Lebanon's two missing laws.** The minister ties the digital economy to a personal data protection law and a cybersecurity law, both still drafts. [Story 1: AI ministry bill]({{ full }}#story-1)
