## This week's two items for you

- **AI as a confidant.** In 29 interviews, young people turned to AI after a specific conflict. It worked as a bridge when they used it to see other views or rehearse a hard conversation, and as a wedge when they used it only to feel validated. [Story 6: AI as a confidant]({{ full }}#story-6)
- **Assessment is changing.** England's university regulator wants more secure assessment, such as in-person exams and vivas. Kuwait University asks students to disclose the AI tool and the prompts they used. [Story 2: AI-free exams]({{ full }}#story-2)

**Opportunities:** MBZUAI in Abu Dhabi takes master's and PhD applications until Dec 15, with funded places paying a monthly stipend. The Global Sustainability Challenge for student teams closes Nov 22. [Opportunities and deadlines]({{ full }}#opportunities)
