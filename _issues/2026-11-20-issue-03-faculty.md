## This week's three items for you
- **Students write for the detector.** In a survey of 5,034 US undergraduates, 25% said they changed how they write to avoid being falsely flagged by AI detectors. [Story 4: Students and AI detectors]({{ full }}#story-4)
- **For faculty who publish: fake references reach a journal's editors.** Taylor & Francis retracted an editorial by the editors of the Journal of Further and Higher Education over references it could not verify. AI use has not been confirmed. [For researchers]({{ full }}#for-researchers)
- **For computing faculty: a US chip rule names Lebanon.** A new US rule requires a licence for data centres that serve companies headquartered in D:5 countries, Lebanon included, above a set threshold. [Story 1: US chip rule]({{ full }}#story-1)

**Opportunities:** the Fulbright Visiting Scholar Program closes Dec 1 and the MOPGA 2027 fellowship closes Dec 15. [Opportunities and deadlines]({{ full }}#opportunities)

## Try this week (15 minutes)

Take the reference list of a draft you are working on and open each DOI to check it leads to the paper you cite.
