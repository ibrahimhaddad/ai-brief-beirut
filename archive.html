Issues are filed by the ISO 8601 week in their label, so a year has 52 or 53 weeks. An issue published in the first days of January can belong to the last week of the year before: the issue of Fri Jan 1, 2027 covers Dec 25 to 31, 2026, so it is Week 53 and is listed under 2026.
