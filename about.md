The AI Brief - LB Edition is a weekly newsletter by [Ibrahim Haddad]({{ '/author/' | relative_url }}). LB is the country code for Lebanon.

## What it is for

Each week it explains what the most important AI developments mean for readers in Lebanon and the region: what happened, why it matters, how it could affect you, and what you could do. It informs. It does not set rules or give instructions for any institution.

## Who it is for

In universities:

- **University staff** who run services and operations.
- **University faculty** who teach, assess, research and publish.
- **University students** building skills and careers.

In business:

- **Entrepreneurs** building new companies in Lebanon and the region.
- **Businesses** of any size in Lebanon and the region working out how to use AI in their operations.

Every issue has a full version and a two-minute page for each audience.

## How it is made

Research and first drafts are prepared with AI tools. Every story, figure and recommendation is checked and approved by the author before publication. Each story names its evidence: research, official sources, reporting or opinion. Links point to the sources used. A red cedar marks stories and items whose main subject is Lebanon.

Suggestions in the newsletter are for readers to consider.

## Independence

This is an independent, personal publication. Views are the author's own.

## Corrections

Errors are fixed in the issue and noted at its end with the date. [Report one here]({{ '/feedback/' | relative_url }}?type=correction).

## Suggest a story

Seen something readers here should know about? [Send it in]({{ '/feedback/' | relative_url }}?type=story). Every tip is checked against its own sources before it runs, and you are thanked by name only if you ask.
