The AI Brief - Beirut Edition is a weekly newsletter by [Ibrahim Haddad]({{ '/author/' | relative_url }}) on what AI developments mean for university staff, faculty, students and founders in Lebanon and the Middle East.

Each issue picks the week's most important developments and answers four questions for each one: what happened, why it matters, how it could affect you, and what you could do.

## Who it is for

- **Staff** who run university services and operations.
- **Faculty** who teach, assess, research and publish.
- **Students** building skills and careers.
- **Entrepreneurs** building companies in Lebanon and the region.

Every issue has a full version and a two-minute page for each audience.

## How it is made

Research and first drafts are prepared with AI tools. Every story, figure and recommendation is checked and approved by the author before publication. Each story names its evidence: research, official sources, reporting or opinion. Links point to the sources used.

Suggestions in the newsletter are for readers to consider.

## Independence

This is an independent publication. Views are the author's own.
